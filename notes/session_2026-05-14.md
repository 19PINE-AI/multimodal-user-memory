## What this means for the paper

The story has become sharper, not weaker:

- **Sanity §1, §2 (encoder soundness): PASS.** ArcFace and ECAPA-TDNN are valid invariance-preserving encoders for the cross-condition identity case. The mechanism is worth building.
- **§3 (in-domain learned-RQ-VAE wins): WITHDRAWN.** The numbers are real but the contribution claim "learned > naive" cannot be supported by them, because they leak identity information from train into test.
- **§4 (held-out classifier-supervised RQ-VAE): FAILS.** Classifier loss biases codebook toward train-id-separating directions; doesn't transfer.

This is a productive negative result: the *right* quantiser is genuinely an open research question, and the paper now has a clean experimental track (contrastive vs reconstruction-only vs augmentation-invariant) rather than a hand-wave.

## What to do next session

In priority order:

1. **Reconstruction-only RQ-VAE.** Drop the classifier loss entirely; let the autoencoder allocate. Compare to naive k-means on held-out identities. Hypothesis: the nonlinear encoder/decoder still helps even without supervision.

2. **Contrastive RQ-VAE.** Generate positive pairs by augmenting the same image / time-shifting the same audio. Train codebook so positive pairs map to the same code more often than chance. No identity labels needed. This is the most principled formulation for the "perceptual identity preservation" objective.

3. **Sanity check 3 (style)** — encoder choice for painter style. CLIP would collapse style into semantics; need a contrastive style head or StyleGAN-encoder features. WikiArt cross-period pairs as the eval set.

4. **Sanity check 4 (acoustic scene)** — PANNs CNN14 features on DCASE TAU cross-recording pairs.

5. **Begin Qwen3-VL + Voxtral wiring** — load both, verify end-to-end multimodal inference works on a couple of trivial cases, *before* adding the Engram module.

## Open questions worth thinking about before the next session

- Is the held-out failure a signal that *any* identity-supervised objective will overfit, or specifically that classifier-cross-entropy does? Maybe identity-margin loss (like ArcFace's own training objective, applied to the codebook) would generalise better.
- Should we measure the "transferred" RQ-VAE more carefully? E.g., the in-domain learned vs the held-out learned: where in the codebook does the overfitting concentrate?
- Is naive k-means actually fine? At eff_K=4096 on audio held-out it gets ratio 31. If we can scale to 1000+ identities with naive RQ at moderate depth, maybe the "learned quantiser" claim isn't load-bearing for the paper — the contribution shifts to the gate/parametric-row insertion side.

## Update: post-session-phase-2 escalation

After the v0 sanity checks passed, I ran the actual mechanism test (`src/engram_retrieval.py`) and it failed. Vision Perceptual Engram retrieval@1 was 0.48 at N=20 vs embedding-RAG cosine NN baseline at 0.96. Audio was 0.60 vs 1.0 baseline. The frozen-codebook + hash-table mechanism is decisively beaten by direct embedding similarity, contradicting the v1 hypothesis that surgical row insertion would be competitive.

I also ran a quantiser bakeoff (`src/quantiser_bakeoff.py`) trying three post-trained variants (reconstruction-only, classifier, contrastive RQ-VAE) against naive k-means. Naive won at the configurations that matter (eff_K ≥ 4096 with productive depth). No post-trained variant beat naive at scale.

**Per user authorisation, escalated to from-scratch multimodal pretraining.** Full architectural plan in `notes/v2_architecture_plan.md`. Strategy:
- Fork `~/user-as-engram/nanochat` to `src/nanochat_mm/`.
- Extend the Engram module with per-modality tables (Path A) keyed by quantised perceptual code tuples.
- Train end-to-end: codebook + gate + LM all see the NTP loss.
- Pretrain budget: ~4B multimodal tokens, ~4-7 days on the Blackwell.
- Critical novel ingredient vs user-as-engram: a "recurrence stream" (Ego4D / VoxConverse / YT-Temporal) where the same identity appears across long-range context — this is what teaches the gate to fire on perceptual recurrence.

The paper framing now: "post-training a frozen-codebook Engram doesn't work; the codebook must be trained jointly with the LM via NTP loss propagation through the gate's straight-through estimator." The v0-v1 negative results become a load-bearing motivation in §2 of the paper instead of an embarrassment.
